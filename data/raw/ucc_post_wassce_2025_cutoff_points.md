## Coverage and caveats

This document covers 54 selected UCC programme entries for the POST-WASSCE
(2025) admission category. It is **not a complete list of all UCC
programmes** offered that cycle.

The cut-off aggregates below were not verified as separate male/female
figures and must not be interpreted as gender-specific cut-offs.

`BSc Physician Assistant Studies` has no published cut-off for this cycle
and is marked `Not published` below rather than estimated.

**Before using these figures for an actual application decision**, they
should be checked against the live official UCC admissions database at
the source URLs above — these are a point-in-time snapshot, not a live
feed.
