# About These Examples

The figures below are specific, dated cut-off points reported for the
2026/2027 Ghanaian university admission cycle, each tied to a named
institution, programme, and source. They are a step more concrete than the
general aggregate bands elsewhere in this system's data, but they are
still **examples from secondary news reporting on university-published
figures**, not a live feed from any university's own admissions system,
and not a guarantee that these exact numbers apply to a future cycle.

An important honesty note, found while compiling this file: two different
2026/27 articles reported different cut-off aggregates for the same KNUST
Medicine programme - one said aggregate 6, another said aggregate 7, both
citing KNUST's own release. This kind of disagreement between sources
reporting on the same real event is common and expected, not a sign one
source is simply wrong. Treat every figure below as "this specific source
reported this specific number for this specific cycle," not as an
undisputed fact, and always recommend the student confirm directly with
the institution before relying on any cut-off point for a real decision.

# University of Ghana, 2026/2027 Cycle

The University of Ghana published cut-off points for undergraduate
admission across all faculties for the 2026/2027 academic year. Some
examples, from the most to least competitive end of the range:

- BSc Biomedical Engineering: aggregate 6 - the single most competitive
  figure UG published that cycle.
- Bachelor of Laws (LLB), BSc Computer Engineering, BSc Computer Science:
  aggregate 7 each.
- MBChB Medicine: aggregate 8.
- BSc Administration, BSc Public Health: aggregate 9 each.
- Bachelor of Dental Surgery, BSc Biomedical Sciences, BSc Information
  Technology, Doctor of Pharmacy: aggregate 10 each.
- BSc Medical Laboratory Science: aggregate 12.
- BSc Radiography: aggregate 13.
- BA Communication Studies: aggregate 14.
- Political Science: aggregate 16.
- BA English, BA Sociology: aggregate 18 each.
- History, African Studies, French, Chinese, Linguistics: aggregate 20 to
  24.

The University of Ghana's general regular-admission ceiling is aggregate
24; Distance Education admission uses a higher ceiling of aggregate 30.
Meeting a programme's cut-off point does not guarantee admission - places
are limited, and admission is also subject to individual performance
relative to other applicants and any programme-specific subject
prerequisites.

# KNUST, 2026/2027 Cycle

KNUST announced entrance exam requirements for Medicine and Dentistry
applicants for the 2026/2027 cycle: an aggregate of 7 or better for
Medicine (BSc Human Biology), and an aggregate of 9 or better for
Dentistry, with required core subjects of English, Core Mathematics, and
Integrated Science (or Social Studies for STEM candidates), alongside
electives in Chemistry, Biology, Physics, or Elective Mathematics.

A separate report of KNUST's 2026/27 cut-off aggregates listed Medicine at
aggregate 6 instead of 7 - see the honesty note above about this
discrepancy between sources.

# Why Cut-Off Points Change Between Cycles

The same University of Ghana BSc Biomedical Engineering programme that
showed an aggregate cut-off of 9 in a cited 2020/21 example elsewhere in
this system's data showed an aggregate of 6 in the 2026/2027 cycle
reported here - a real, sourced illustration of how much a specific
programme's cut-off can move from one admission cycle to the next, driven
by that cycle's number of applicants and available places. A cut-off
point from any past cycle, verified or not, should never be treated as a
prediction of a future cycle's cut-off.
